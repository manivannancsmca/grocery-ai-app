# Selling as a vendor or restaurant

## Registration and approval
Register as a grocery vendor or a restaurant owner and complete the store profile. A grocery store needs its business
and legal name, GSTIN, contacts and an address with its map location (an FSSAI number is optional). A restaurant needs
its name, cuisines, FSSAI number, contacts, address and opening hours. Submit the profile for approval: it goes from
draft to pending approval, then approved or rejected with a reason. You can edit the profile while it is a draft or
after a rejection, then submit it again. An approved store can be suspended by the platform and reinstated later.

## Products and menu
Approved grocery vendors create products as drafts and then publish, unpublish or archive them. Restaurants can build
their menu before approval, mark dishes sold out and pause new orders.

## Stock and prices
Grocery vendors raise, lower or set stock with a reason, and set a low-stock alert level. A product's first price
applies at once; a later small change also applies at once, while a large change waits for an admin's approval.
Restaurant menu prices are changed directly.

## The order board
New orders appear on the order board. Accept an order with a ready time, or reject it with a reason (out of stock,
store closed, too busy, or other with a note), then mark it packing or preparing, and ready for pickup. You have a
limited time to accept a new order; if you do not respond in time it is declined automatically and the customer is
refunded.

## Returns, reviews, reports and purchasing
Approve or reject customers' return requests with a comment, and reply once to each review. Sales reports cover up to
92 days. Grocery vendors can keep suppliers and purchase orders: draft, send, receive (which adds the stock), close or
cancel.

## Payouts
The app has no payout, settlement or commission feature: questions about payments to your business are handled outside
the app.
