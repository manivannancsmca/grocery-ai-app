# Delivery partner guide

## Joining as a delivery partner
Register as a delivery partner, then complete your profile: name, phone, city and vehicle type. Unless you ride a
bicycle, add your vehicle number and driving licence too. Submit the profile; an admin or a delivery manager approves
it. You can use the partner features once you are approved.

## Going online and sharing your location
Go online to receive delivery offers and offline when you stop. You cannot go offline during an active delivery. While
you are online the app shares your location regularly, also when you stand still. If your location is too old, you do
not receive offers, so keep the app open and location access allowed.

## Receiving offers
Offers go to approved partners who are online, free, have no other pending offer and a recent location. An offer shows
the store, the drop-off area, the distances and the number of items; the customer's details appear only after you
accept. Each offer has a time limit; accept or reject it in the app.

## Picking up and delivering
After accepting, go to the store. You can mark the order picked up only after the store has marked it ready. Then
deliver it and mark it delivered. If you cannot deliver it, choose "Couldn't deliver" with the reason: customer
unreachable, address not found, refused, or other. If an order is cancelled before you pick it up, the app tells you
not to pick it up.

## History and earnings
The app shows your delivery history and a summary. The earnings figures shown are estimates for your information; the
app does not handle partner payments.
