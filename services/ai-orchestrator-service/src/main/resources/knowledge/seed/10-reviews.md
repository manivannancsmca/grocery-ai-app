# Reviews and ratings

## Who can review
Customers can review a delivered order within the review window, which starts at delivery and is set by the platform's
admins. For one order you can review each item, the store or restaurant, and the delivery partner, once each.

## Writing a review
A review has 1 to 5 stars, a title and a comment. You can edit it for a short time after posting and delete it at any
time.

## Reviews that are checked first
A review with links, phone numbers, email addresses, rude words or mostly capital letters stays hidden until an admin
has looked at it.

## Replies from stores
The store or restaurant can post one public reply to a review. Reviews show the reviewer's first name and initial.
