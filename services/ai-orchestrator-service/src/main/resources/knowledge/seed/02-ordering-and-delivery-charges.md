# Ordering, minimum order and delivery charges

## How ordering works
Add items to your cart, open the checkout preview, choose a delivery address, check the total, then place the order and
pay for it online on the order page (there is no cash on delivery). A cart, and so an order, is always from one store or restaurant: adding an item from a
different store asks whether you want to start a new cart. You can add 1 to 20 of each item and up to 30 different
items. Prices, stock and opening hours are checked again when you place the order; nothing is held for you before that.

## What can stop a checkout
The checkout preview tells you if something needs your attention: an item is out of stock, the store is closed or
paused, the address is outside the delivery area, the order is below the minimum order amount, or no address with a map
location is chosen. If the total changed since you looked at the preview, you are asked to confirm the new total.

## Delivery charges
The delivery fee depends on the distance from the store to your address: a base fee covers the first few kilometres,
and each extra started kilometre adds a small charge. Grocery orders above a certain amount are delivered free.
Restaurant orders have no free delivery. The exact fee of your order is shown in the checkout preview, and an order
keeps the fee it had when it was placed.

## Minimum order amount
Grocery orders have a minimum order amount; restaurant orders have none. The platform's admins set the minimum order,
the delivery fees and the free-delivery amount, and they can change them, so they are not written in this article: the
assistant reads the amounts in force from the app, and the checkout preview shows them for your cart.

## When the store accepts
After you pay, the store or restaurant has a limited time to accept the order. If it does not respond in time, the order
is cancelled automatically and you get a full refund. A store can also decline an order, for example when an item is out
of stock, the store is closed or too busy; a paid order is then refunded in full.
