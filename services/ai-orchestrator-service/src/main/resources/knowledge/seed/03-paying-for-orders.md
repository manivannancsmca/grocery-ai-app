# Paying for your order and payment methods

## When you pay
You pay right after placing the order, before the store accepts it. The order page shows a pay-by time; pay with the
"Pay now" button on the order page, which opens the secure payment window. If the time runs out, the order is
cancelled automatically, the items are released and nothing is charged. If a payment is declined, the order keeps
waiting and you can try again while the pay-by time has not passed.

## Payment methods
All payments are online, in Indian rupees, through Razorpay's secure payment window. Razorpay offers the methods, such
as cards, UPI, net banking and wallets.

## No cash on delivery
The app does not accept cash on delivery (COD) or any payment at the door: every order is paid online before the store
accepts it.

## Payments and safety
The assistant never takes payments in the chat and nobody from the app ever asks for your card number, CVV, UPI PIN,
OTP or password. Do not share them with anyone.

## A payment that arrives late
If money is taken after an order was already cancelled (for example because the pay-by time had passed), it is refunded
automatically to the same payment method.

## Coupons
You can use one coupon per cart: type its code or pick one from the offers list. A coupon can take a percentage or a
fixed amount off, or make the delivery free. Coupons can have their own rules, such as a minimum order value, dates,
or a limit on how often each customer may use them. If a coupon no longer applies, the checkout shows it and you can
remove it. When an order is cancelled, the coupon use is given back.
