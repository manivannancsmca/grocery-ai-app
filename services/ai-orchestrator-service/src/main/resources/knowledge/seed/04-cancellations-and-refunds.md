# Cancellations and refunds

## Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel an order yourself until the store accepts it: while it is waiting for payment, or paid and waiting for
the store. Once the store has accepted the order you cannot cancel it yourself; raise a complaint and the support team
will help. Nobody can cancel an order after the delivery partner has picked it up. The order page and the assistant
show whether your order can still be cancelled.

## What happens to my money when an order is cancelled
If you had not paid yet, nothing is charged. If you had paid, the full amount is refunded automatically to the same
payment method, and the items go back to the store's stock.

## Automatic refunds
A paid order is refunded in full automatically when you cancel it, when the store declines it or does not accept it in
time, when an admin cancels it, or when a payment arrives after the order was already cancelled.

## Refunds that are not automatic
If a delivery fails (for example the address could not be found), there is no automatic refund: the support team
decides and contacts you about it. Admins can also give a full or partial refund for other problems.

## Where to see a refund
The order page shows the refund and its status: refund pending, refunded or partially refunded. Refunds of returned
items also appear under Account, Returns. How long the bank takes to show the money depends on the bank and the payment
method; the payment assistant can tell you the usual time. A refund that fails is tried again automatically.
