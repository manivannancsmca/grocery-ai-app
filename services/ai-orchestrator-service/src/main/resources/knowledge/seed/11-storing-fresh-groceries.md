# Storing fresh groceries

## Leafy greens and herbs
Keep spinach, coriander, mint and other leafy greens in the refrigerator, dry and loosely wrapped in a cloth or paper
towel inside a container or bag. Wash them just before use, not before storing, and use them within a few days.

## Fruit and vegetables at room temperature
Keep bananas, whole mangoes until ripe, and tomatoes until ripe at room temperature, away from direct sunlight. Store
potatoes and onions in a cool, dark, airy place, and keep them apart from each other: together they spoil faster.

## Fruit and vegetables in the refrigerator
Ripe fruit, cut fruit and most other vegetables (such as carrots, beans, cauliflower and capsicum) keep longer in the
refrigerator's vegetable drawer. Keep cut fruit and vegetables covered and use them soon.

## Milk, curd and paneer
Refrigerate milk, curd and paneer as soon as they arrive and keep them closed. Boiled milk should cool before it goes
into the refrigerator. Use them by the date on the pack.

## Bread, frozen food and dry goods
Keep bread in its bag at room temperature for a few days, or freeze it for longer. Put frozen food into the freezer
straight away and do not refreeze food that has thawed. Keep rice, flour, lentils and spices in airtight containers in a
cool, dry place.

## Check the pack
Always follow the storage instructions and the use-by date printed on the product's pack: they come first.
