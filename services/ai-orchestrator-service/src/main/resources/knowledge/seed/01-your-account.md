# Your account and signing in

## Creating an account
You register with your email address, a password, your full name and, if you like, a phone number. You also choose
what you are joining as: a customer, a grocery vendor, a restaurant owner or a delivery partner. The password must be
8 to 64 characters long and contain at least one letter and one digit.

## Signing in
You sign in with your email address and password. The app has no Google or other social sign-in and no sign-in with a
one-time code (OTP) or phone number. After several wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for a while, to
protect it; wait a little and try again, or reset the password.

## Forgot your password
Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. The app emails you a reset link that works
once and only for a short time. For your safety the page says the same thing whether or not an account exists for that
address. Resetting the password signs you out everywhere; then sign in with the new password. Changing your password
while signed in signs out your other devices.

## Your profile and addresses
Customers can change their name and phone number on the profile page. The email address cannot be changed. You can
keep up to 10 delivery addresses: add, edit or delete them and choose the default one. Your first address becomes the
default. An address needs its location on the map before it can be used for a delivery.

## Deleting an account
The app has no way to delete an account yourself. If you need help with your account, raise a complaint in the "App"
category under Account, Help & support, and the support team will answer you there.
