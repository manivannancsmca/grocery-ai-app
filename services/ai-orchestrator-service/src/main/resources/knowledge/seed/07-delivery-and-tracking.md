# Delivery and order tracking

## Order statuses in plain words
An order is placed, then waits for payment, then is confirmed (paid). The store accepts it and prepares it (shown as
"Packing" for groceries), marks it ready for pickup, the delivery partner picks it up and it is out for delivery, and
finally delivered. An order can also end as declined by the store, cancelled, or delivery failed.

## How a delivery partner is found
When the store accepts your order, the app offers the delivery to the nearest available delivery partners, a few at a
time, and widens the search step by step. The first partner to accept gets the delivery. If nobody is found yet, the
search keeps trying and the operations team is alerted.

## Tracking your order
From the moment a partner accepts until your order is delivered, the order page shows live tracking: a map, the
estimated arrival time, and the partner's first name and vehicle. The partner's phone number and vehicle number are
shown only while that partner has your delivery.

## Delivery area
Stores deliver within a limited distance. The checkout preview tells you if your address is outside a store's delivery
area.

## If a delivery fails
If the partner cannot deliver (for example you could not be reached, the address was not found, or the order was
refused), the order becomes "delivery failed". There is no automatic refund: the support team looks at it and contacts
you about a refund.
