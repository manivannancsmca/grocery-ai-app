# Complaints and customer support

## How to contact customer support
Customer support works through complaints: open Account, Help & support and raise a complaint, or ask the Customer
support assistant to raise one for you (it files it only after you agree). The app has no phone line, email address or
live chat with an agent.

## Complaint categories
Choose the category that fits: Delivery (late or missing delivery, the delivery partner), Payment (charged but not
confirmed, charged twice, a refund that has not arrived), Items (quality), Store (the store's or restaurant's service),
App (the app does not work), or Other. For damaged, wrong or missing items, ask for a return instead: that is what
refunds items.

## What to write
Linking the complaint to an order is optional. Describe what happened in 10 to 1000 characters. You can have one open
complaint per order and a few open complaints at a time.

## What happens next
A complaint is open, then in progress, then resolved. The support team writes its answer on the complaint, and you see
it under Help & support. You are not notified by email or SMS about complaints; check Help & support or ask the
Customer support assistant whether support has answered.
