# Product photo credits

Product photos used under the licenses below. They illustrate the local development data:

- the grocery catalog in `infrastructure/local/seed/catalog/` (loaded by
  `infrastructure/local/scripts/seed-catalog.mjs`, and partly by the Chennai stores). Each product's photo is named in
  `products.csv`, and each category shows one of its products' photos;
- the smoke tests' products (assigned by `infrastructure/local/scripts/seed-product-images.sh`);
- the Chennai stores' branded products: see [Chennai branded products](#chennai-branded-products) below.

The first table's photos come from Wikimedia Commons; each file is the Commons 960 px wide thumbnail of the original,
unedited. A photo there that shows a brand shows it only as part of the original picture; the catalog's products are
unbranded. Vendors' own photos replace any of them per product.
